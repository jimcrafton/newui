Blue bar = new in M4. Everything else is unchanged.

Cut/Copy put the selection on the system clipboard as tab-separated text (pastes into Excel, Notepad...). Paste puts the clipboard's top-left cell at the active cell. One copied cell pasted over a selection fills it.

Fill Down/Right copy the top row / left column of the selection over the rest (one row selected: copies from the row above / column to the left).

Each is one undo step. The status bar reports the result:

Pasted B2:D5 - 12 cells changed  |  "Pasted A1:C3 - the part past the last column was dropped"  |  "C4: Enter a whole number"
